What Does a SOC Analyst Do?

A beginner-friendly guide from Learn Cyber Daily.

A Security Operations Center (SOC) monitors an organization's environment, investigates suspicious activity, and helps coordinate response to security incidents.

A typical alert workflow

  1. Validate the alert and understand its detection logic.
  2. Gather context: user, host, time, source, destination, and related events.
  3. Correlate evidence across logs and approved tools.
  4. Classify the finding as benign, suspicious, or confirmed malicious based on evidence.
  5. Document findings, escalate according to playbooks, and preserve evidence.

Useful skills

Networking, Windows/Linux logs, SIEM queries, analytical thinking, clear writing, and knowing when to escalate. Never close an alert solely because an AI says it is benign.

Learning note: use these ideas only in authorized environments. Keep practicing and verify your sources.
