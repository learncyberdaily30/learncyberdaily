Wireshark for Beginners

A beginner-friendly guide from Learn Cyber Daily.

Wireshark is a network protocol analyzer used to inspect packet captures and understand communication between systems.

Begin safely

Use a capture from your own device or an authorized lab. Avoid collecting traffic from networks or people without permission; captures may contain sensitive data.

Basic workflow

  1. Open an authorized PCAP file or select an approved interface.
  2. Start or load the capture.
  3. Use display filters to focus on protocols or endpoints.
  4. Select a packet to inspect its protocol layers.
  5. Follow a stream only when appropriate and redact sensitive details before sharing.

Remember: a display filter changes what you view, not what was captured.

Learning note: use these ideas only in authorized environments. Keep practicing and verify your sources.
