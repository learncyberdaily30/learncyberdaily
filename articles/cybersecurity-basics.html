What Is Cybersecurity?

A beginner-friendly guide from Learn Cyber Daily.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, disruption, or misuse.

The three core goals: CIA triad

  • Confidentiality: only authorized people can access information.
  • Integrity: information remains accurate and unaltered.
  • Availability: systems and data are accessible when needed.

Common areas

Network security, endpoint security, application security, identity and access management, cloud security, and security operations all contribute to a defense-in-depth strategy.

How to begin

Learn networking fundamentals, operating systems, basic scripting, and security concepts. Practice in legal, intentionally vulnerable labs; document what you learn and verify claims from AI tools.

Learning note: use these ideas only in authorized environments. Keep practicing and verify your sources.
